Headbang recorder

Records head movement with the phone's motion sensors, waits in silence, then plays the music through the phone speaker.

The sample tracks are made in the browser (drums and distorted guitar), so their beat times are exact. The first beat is at 0 s.

Use your own music files instead

For each file, enter its tempo and the time of its first beat in the file, so the beat times can be calculated.

On iPhone: turn off silent mode, turn the volume all the way up, and tap Allow when the page asks for motion access.

Waiting for motion data…

Strap the phone on the participant's head (upright, screen facing out), then tap Start. Recording starts right away; the music follows after the silence.
