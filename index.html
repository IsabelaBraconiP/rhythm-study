    Finger-tapping task

    You'll hear a steady click. Tap along with it, in time, by pressing the Space bar or tapping the large circle.

    After a while the clicks stop. Keep tapping at the same speed until the screen says Done.

    • —Start tapping as soon as you've found the beat.
    • —Use one finger of your dominant hand and keep the movement relaxed.

    Tapping results

    Clicks and taps

    Each round on a timeline: when the clicks played and when you tapped.

    What do these numbers mean?
    Asynchrony
    : average time between your tap and the click. Negative means you tapped slightly before the click; most people do this by a few tens of milliseconds.
    SD
    : standard deviation: how much that timing varied from tap to tap. Lower is more consistent.
    Variability
    : coefficient of variation (CV): variation in the time between your taps, as a percentage of their average. Lower is steadier.
    Tempo after clicks stopped
    : your tapping speed once the clicks stopped, and how much faster (+) or slower (−) that was than the original beat.
